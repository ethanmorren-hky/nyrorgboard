The market

Average player price by position

Each player's price is the cap hit the free-agent market would pay this season for how he is expected to play. It moves after every game he plays, when contracts signed elsewhere change what teams pay, and when the cap rises. The index lines below average today's NHL players by position.

Team portfolios

Market value vs cap spent, NHL rosters

A team's portfolio is the sum of its players' prices. Value over cap is this season's market value minus this season's cap hits; contract surplus adds up every remaining year, with each player aged forward.

Market news

New contracts and rate moves

When a contract is signed or changed anywhere in the league, it joins the data the market rate is fitted on. A rich deal for a defenseman raises what every comparable defenseman is worth, which makes existing contracts look better.

    Signed, not yet in effect

    These deals are signed but start next season or later. Each is graded the same way as a live contract: his projected price in every season of the new deal, aged forward and grown with the cap, minus the new cap hit.
