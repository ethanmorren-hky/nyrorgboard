What these stats mean
Expected goals (xG)
MoneyPuck's estimate of how likely each shot was to score, from its location, type, angle, rebound and rush context. Adding them up measures chance quality, not just volume.
xG share (xGF%)
The team's share of all expected goals while on the ice. Above 50% means it is out-chancing opponents.
Corsi and Fenwick
Shot attempt share: Corsi counts all attempts, Fenwick leaves out blocked ones.
xG share vs teammates (Rel)
The team's xG share with the player on the ice minus without him. It filters out how good the team is overall.
Finishing
Goals scored minus expected goals. Positive means shooters converted more than average shooters would on the same chances.
Goals saved above expected (GSAx)
Expected goals faced minus goals allowed. Positive means the goalie stopped more than an average goalie would have.
Game score
MoneyPuck's single-number summary of a player's contribution in a game, built from goals, assists, shots, chances, penalties and faceoffs.

How to use the Ops Board

  • Click any player to open his contract, stats and the moves available to him. A side panel opens; close it with the × to give the rosters full width.
  • Drag players between slots to change lines and pairs, or between the Rangers and Wolf Pack boards to start a recall or assignment.
  • Build a trade to pick a partner team and search its roster. Contracts, clauses and stats fill in from the league database. The League contracts table at the bottom has a Trade for button on every player.
  • Every move shows its impact on cap space, roster, contracts and future seasons, with 2025 CBA checks, before you add it to the scenario.
  • Scenario moves in the toolbar lists what you've added; remove any move to undo it. Scenarios are saved in this browser only.
  • Roster updates under the boards scroll the latest Rangers moves, or the whole league. Data refreshes every morning.
