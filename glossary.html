The Glossary

One definition for every stat on the site. Counted = from the official scorer or contract record. Tracked = measured by tracking (NHL EDGE, All Three Zones). Model = an estimate; these carry a small "model" tag wherever they appear.

Contracts and cap

TermTypeWhat it means
Cap hitAAV / cap hitCountedWhat a contract counts against the salary cap each season: total salary and signing bonuses divided by years.
Cap chargeCountedWhat actually counts against this team's cap after adjustments: retained salary, buried AHL relief ($1.225M off for players in the minors), LTIR. Can differ from cap hit (e.g. Korpisalo: $4.0M hit, $3.0M charge after 25% retained).
Cap spaceCountedUpper limit ($104.0M in 2026-27) minus the team's total cap charge.
Retained salaryRetentionCountedShare of a traded player's cap hit his old team keeps paying (max 50%, max three retained contracts per team).
BuriedCountedA player on a one-way NHL deal sent to the minors; his cap hit minus $1.225M still counts.
Performance bonusesPerf. bonusesCountedExtra pay for hitting games-played or scoring targets; count against the cap when earned and can carry over to next season.
ExpiryExpiry / UFA yearCountedThe last season of the contract. "UFA" = can sign anywhere after it; "RFA" = team keeps rights if it makes a qualifying offer.
ExtensionCountedA new contract signed before the current one ends; starts the season after.
Entry-level contractELCCountedA first NHL contract for young players, capped in salary and length.
No-move / no-tradeNMC / NTCCountedClauses that stop a team from moving (NMC) or trading (NTC, full or partial) a player without his consent.
Waiver-exemptCountedCan be sent to the minors without other teams getting a chance to claim him.

Results and scoring

TermTypeWhat it means
Points %P%CountedStandings points earned divided by points available.
Goal differentialGDCountedGoals scored minus goals allowed.
Goals shareGF%CountedShare of all goals while a player or team is on the ice that his side scored.
Shooting %Sh%CountedGoals divided by shots on goal.
Save %Sv%CountedSaves divided by shots faced.
PDOCountedOn-ice shooting % plus save %. Far above or below 100 usually means luck that won't last.
Ice timeTOICountedMinutes played. "/60" means per 60 minutes of ice time, so players with different minutes compare fairly.
5v5 / power play / penalty kill5v5 / PP / PKCountedEven strength, man advantage, and shorthanded situations. Most analytics use 5v5 because it's the largest, fairest sample.

Shots and chances

TermTypeWhat it means
Shot attemptsCorsi (CF/CA)CountedEvery shot at the net: on goal, missed or blocked. A volume and possession signal.
Shot attempt shareCF%CountedShare of all shot attempts (for + against) that his side took. Above 50% = outshooting the other team.
Unblocked attemptsFenwickCountedShot attempts minus blocked shots.
Expected goalsxGModelHow many goals a set of shots "should" produce, given where and how each was taken (distance, angle, type, rebound, rush). Source: MoneyPuck model.
Expected goals sharexGF%ModelShare of expected goals (for + against) created by his side. Better than shot share at predicting future results.
Relative xGRel xGF%ModelHow much better his team's expected-goals share is with him on the ice than without him.
Goals above expectedGAx / finishingModelActual goals minus expected goals. Positive = finished better than the chances suggested; only partly repeatable.
Goals saved above expectedGSAxModelExpected goals against minus goals allowed for a goalie. The best single goalie number.
High-danger chancesHDCFModelShots from the most dangerous areas (close, in the slot). Definitions vary by source; ours is MoneyPuck's.
Game scoreModelOne-number summary of a player's game from goals, assists, shots, blocks, penalties and on-ice results (MoneyPuck).
Win probabilityWin %ModelChance a team wins from the current score, time and chances so far (MoneyPuck, per game).

Transition and microstats (tracked)

TermTypeWhat it means
Zone entryEntries /60TrackedBringing the puck into the offensive zone. "Controlled" = carried or passed in; dump-ins don't count. Source: All Three Zones (hand-tracked, about a third of games).
Controlled entry %CE%TrackedShare of entries made with possession. Controlled entries produce about twice the shots of dump-ins.
Entry denialDenial rateTrackedDefender stops an entry attempt at the blue line.
Zone exitExits with possession %TrackedGetting the puck out of the defensive zone with control, not just clearing it.
Forecheck pressurePressures /60TrackedForcing a defender into a rushed play or turnover in his own end.
Shot assistsShot assists /60TrackedPasses that directly set up a shot, including those that didn't become goals.
Skating speed / burstsMax speed, 20+ mph burstsTrackedNHL EDGE puck-and-player tracking: top speed and how often a player skates over 20 mph.
Offensive-zone timeOZ time %TrackedShare of even-strength time the play is in the opponent's end (NHL EDGE).

Value and contracts (The Market)

TermTypeWhat it means
ImpactGAR/82ModelGoals above a replacement-level player per 82 games, built from even-strength offense and defense, power play, penalty kill and penalties. This project's own scale (not Evolving-Hockey's).
PriceMarket valueModelThe cap hit the current market would pay for his expected play, fitted on every non-entry-level contract in the league, as % of the cap.
Value over capModelThis season's price minus his cap hit.
Contract surplusSurplusModelPrice minus cap hit added up over every remaining season, with age effects.
Contract gradeGradeModelSurplus per season as % of cap: Steal ≥ +3%, Good ≥ +1%, Fair within ±1%, Over ≤ −1%, Burden ≤ −3%.

The Iceberg

TermTypeWhat it means
PercentilePctlCountedWhere a team or player ranks against the league: 90th = better than 90% of them.
Link to winningrModelHow strongly a stat moves with points % across the league (correlation, −1 to 1). Higher = matters more for winning.
Expected points %xP%ModelPoints % a team would have earned if goals had matched expected goals.
